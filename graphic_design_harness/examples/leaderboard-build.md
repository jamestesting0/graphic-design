# How the selected leaderboard was built

Use this as a worked example of the production order. The source file's paint order is a separate decision: a wake built in the last session can still belong behind a figure built yesterday. Open the [editable master](../../final/04-leaderboard/master.svg) beside the [populated poster](../../final/04-leaderboard/populated.png) and its [Discord placement](../../final/04-leaderboard/discord.png).

| Build decision | How the work was done | What to inspect or toggle |
|---|---|---|
| Keep a real place behind the duel | The approved Capital architecture and waterfall gave the picture its distance. Removing unwanted figures did not authorize removing the islands and bridges. The supplied font established the title footprint early. | In the master, start with `distant-capital`, `sky-tone` and `lower-tone`. Compare architectural landmarks with the original at the same crop. The separate `title-assembly` can be hidden to inspect what lies behind it. |
| Resolve the two lead figures | Set the wizards' scale, hand line and gap in relation to each other and to the board. Leave headroom for aircraft. Correct the flat studio lighting in context. The user wanted more than recognizable characters pasted onto scenery. | Compare with `spell-and-reflected-light` hidden. The gesture and depth must still read. Restore the cast and identify which palms and cuffs it actually lights. |
| Pass a foreground layer across intact figures | The left robe developed a blue wedge beside the board. Fading the body into the sky produced an ambiguous edge. The user steered the work toward a separate cloud that crossed the robe continuously. Several cleaner versions still looked too regular. | Toggle `foreground-cloud` and look at both hems. Compare cloud edge scale and irregularity with the painted clouds already in the scene. Pin the user's selected W before trying another mask. [Cloud history](../../outputs/review/variants/wizard-duel/clouds/README.md) |
| Make the reading surface work | The four ranks needed a recognizable order, names, ratings and eventually replaceable portraits. Gold crown, silver shield and bronze medal distinguished podium places by silhouette as well as color. The user chose plain Elo digits and separately available trophy art. | Open `field-components` and toggle `example-data`. Change a name and swap a portrait. Inspect all four rows at posted width. Sample portraits can cover the real replacements if both are visible. [Final component instructions](../../final/README.md) |
| Populate depth one group at a time | A banked dragon approached from the mountain. The spectator ship, balloon crews, barrels, fortress and gliders filled distinct spatial roles. The user compared cast sizes, selected the six-figure study, then chose a 16% dragon enlargement in a later scale study. | The master has groups for each formation. Hide one and ask what becomes easier or harder to read. Check the middle balloon's basket against its canopy and the neighboring aircraft. Preserve the favored lead when editing a wingman. |
| Add light where the action causes it | Separate groups placed sparks at impacts and reflected light beneath each palm. At small size, added cloud branches sometimes made the event less clear. The user kept the preferred arrangement and changed the local light instead. | Toggle `impact-spark-streaks`, `impact-fine-particles`, `aura-left` and `aura-right`. Compare the strength of the reflection with the central clash and restore detail in the receiving cloud. [Palm Light comparison](../../outputs/review/variants/wizard-duel/wizard-aura/council/README.md) |
| Explain movement and preserve hierarchy | The dragon, fortress, glider, spectator ship and two Rocket Balloons each received an adjustable flight treatment. Rear exhaust followed the second nozzle. The rotor treatment retained recognizable blades. The last caster pass detached a bright fragment visually from the left cuff. | Expand `flight-motion` to solo all six passes. Check each wake against its own axis and the nearest silhouette. Toggle `floating-charge-left` and `floating-charge-right` under `floating-spell-particles`. Recheck all four ranking rows in the small preview. ### The late change that reopened the lighting pass

The user liked the stance and wanted more natural shoulders on the viewer-right wizard. A full-figure fit moved the casting gesture toward the audience. An existing reflection also carried faint outlines of the old fingers. The next pass described the approved pose with normalized landmarks and fitted regenerated upper-arm and sleeve details locally. That solved the shoulder objection. It also preserved a spell contact the user subsequently found off-center. After the user selected a particular full figure, the artist fitted that donor and re-registered **both** emitters. The user chose Centered Palms as final. Inspect its unlit and lit hands together; in the short layout also check where the lowest fingertip approaches the board bevel. [Shoulder comparison](../../outputs/review/variants/wizard-duel/right-wizard/Original-Stance/comparison.png) · [Final contact proof](../../outputs/review/variants/wizard-duel/right-wizard/Centered-Palms/detail.png)

For a different commission, replace the duel with its primary gesture or message, the island landmarks with its protected background, the board with its information surface, and the spell with the event that motivates light or motion. Keep the order of decisions and choose the number of components the new work actually needs. A typographic poster may need only a substrate, a title, information groups and one ink treatment. The same process still lets an editor change the title and inspect its actual print size.
