# Review calibration lab

Use these cases with the visual review skill. Open the inputs first and write a verdict, the region, the comparison that decided it, and the smallest correction. Then uncover the historical outcome. For a paired exercise, keep the outcome and maker's ratings from the reviewer until observations are recorded. Paths lead to existing project fixtures. The skills themselves can travel without these assets.

Repeat on the outer cupped hand. The shorter export brings the lowest fingertip close to the board. Preserve that qualification even after both cores pass. ### 8. Portable blind exercise for a flat graphic

Open the [three wayfinding proofs](transfer-proof.svg). The top row is the selected parent. The revision brief moves the terminal stop one place to the right, extends the route to it, and retains a single amber status light centered in the stop. The label and intermediate stop are locked. Judge A and B separately before opening the answer. Hand only the SVG and this brief to a fresh reviewer. This authored exercise travels with the harness.

<details><summary>Expected finding</summary>

Return both candidates. A registers the new light correctly but leaves a second amber dot at the former endpoint. That can be misread as another active stop. Remove the stale light at its source. B clears the stale dot but moves the new light 5 units right and 3 units up from the center of the terminal ring. “Inside the ring” fails the centering requirement. Register the core to the circle center and recheck the single-light count. Both preserve the label. The same two failure modes appeared in the Banner lighting pass.

</details>

### Transfer exercise for a timed deliverable

This is a proposed exercise. Prepare a six-second event title with one supplied licensed track. Compare two exports with the same picture. Shift the impact cue past the reveal in one, and trim the musical tail mid-decay in the other. Ask a reviewer to mark what they **hear**, the timecode, and what they **see** at the cue. The corrective pass moves the first cue to the actual reveal frame and gives the second a clean decay or a deliberate loop. Recheck both on exported playback. The studio archive supplies visual calibration only.

### Replay the mechanical checks

Run these from the studio root with Python and Pillow 10 or newer. The fixtures stay read-only.

```sh
scripts=graphic_design_harness/.agents/skills/design-proof-review/scripts
environment=outputs/environment-03/review
bracket=outputs/review/variants/bracket/abilities

python3 "$scripts/detail_review.py" "$environment/pass-1/environment.png" --record "$environment/pass-1/art.json"
python3 "$scripts/detail_review.py" "$environment/pass-2/environment.png" --record "$environment/pass-1/art.json"
python3 "$scripts/check_edit_scope.py" "$bracket/Y/proof.png" "$bracket/AB/proof.png" --allow 168 219 1170 958
python3 "$scripts/check_edit_scope.py" "$bracket/Y/proof.png" "$bracket/AB/proof.png" --allow 2066 219 3068 958
```

Observed results in order: complete coverage for the **revise** record, rejection of the same record on a different render, matching protected pixels for the actual Miner edit, and a locked-pixel violation when only the opposite side was permitted. Neither helper rates the design.
